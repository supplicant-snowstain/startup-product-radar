# Afterall Photo

- First observed by radar: 2026-11-19 (Europe/Paris)
- Source event/observation: 2026-11-18; do not equate this with first launch date
- Primary source: https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/afterall-photo-before-after/id6814640472
- Evidence: OBSERVED source listing; CLAIMED vendor features and pricing; UNKNOWN independent retention, revenue, PMF and funding

## Job to be done / workflow / alternative / AI role / pricing

Gardeners, contractors and restorers need before/after photographs taken from comparable angles. The iPhone app overlays the reference photo as a transparent ghost, matches zoom and provides a tilt guide; exports GIF, video and PDF reports. Non-AI. Free tier and one-time Pro purchase (€3.99 in France at observation). App Store lists v1.0 and v1.1 in the last few days; no substantial ratings/reviews. Privacy and zero-server claims are developer disclosures, not independently audited. Risk: niche, low repeat frequency.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Interview at least five relevant users and observe at least three independently complete a task using the product versus their existing method; measure errors, task time and willingness to repeat/pay. If switching costs exceed measured gains, reject differentiation.

## Counterevidence and evidence gaps

Marketing descriptions and launch comments are not independent retention data. Funding and VC thesis: no dated verified investment announcement found in this observation window; **unknown**, not necessarily unfunded. Recheck source and user reviews in future iterations.
