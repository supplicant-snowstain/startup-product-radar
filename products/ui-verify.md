## Job to be done / workflow / alternative / AI role / pricing

Coding-agent users need visual regression checks on screens indirectly affected by code changes. Uses snapshots and integration with Playwright, Storybook and other test stacks to show agents affected screens. AI role: agents interpret diffs and propose fixes; deterministic screenshots supply evidence. Free for open source with stated 50k snapshots/month. Founder claims company usage; maker estimates 2–3 min median for affected stories in a ~1k Storybook; neither independently benchmarked. Users question intentional diffs and human approval burden.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Interview at least five relevant users and observe at least three independently complete a task using the product versus their existing method; measure errors, task time and willingness to repeat/pay. If switching costs exceed measured gains, reject differentiation.
