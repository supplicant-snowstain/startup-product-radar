## Job to be done / workflow / alternative / AI role / pricing

Frontend teams need deterministic visual regression coverage for motion, not just still frames. Existing screenshot tests freeze animations; Happo seeks exact animation timestamps and compares APNG frames. No AI is required. Free animated snapshots through 2026; long-term price not verified. Limitations: no animated GIF/WebP/APNG images or view transitions; some canvas loops require drivers. No independently verified retention or growth. Product iteration, not a new company launch.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Interview at least five relevant users and observe at least three independently complete a task using the product versus their existing method; measure errors, task time and willingness to repeat/pay. If switching costs exceed measured gains, reject differentiation.
