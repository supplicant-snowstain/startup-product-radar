## Job to be done / workflow / alternative / AI role / pricing

Small partner-led businesses need attribution from introductions to invoices and referral commissions. Captures email forwards, links and partner-portal submissions, tracks deal stage and Stripe payouts. AI is not necessary to the core workflow; optional automation appears on marketing pages. Free option; usage-based active referral pricing with $99/month example for 25 referrals. Founder Kevin Chern says the system began as an internal tool, then other operators requested it. Site metrics and testimonials are vendor claims, not independently verified. Risks: CRM competition and commission disputes.

## Falsifiable hypothesis

Interview at least five relevant users and observe at least three independently complete a task using the product versus their existing method; measure errors, task time and willingness to repeat/pay. If switching costs exceed measured gains, reject differentiation.

## Counterevidence and evidence gaps

Marketing descriptions and launch comments are not independent retention data. Funding and VC thesis: no dated verified investment announcement found in this observation window; **unknown**, not necessarily unfunded. Recheck source and user reviews in future iterations.
