# Public Place Reality Check — 2026-11-19

**Status:** falsifiable hypothesis, not proven demand. **Evidence:** [Yetly public negative feedback](https://www.producthunt.com/products/yetly), observed 2026-11-19. A commenter reported private fenced gardens and institutional buildings recommended as public activities; founder acknowledged filtering gaps but shipping of a fix is unverified.

**Target/JTBD:** map and activity app developers need to avoid suggesting places that are not publicly accessible. Category tags alone do not establish access rights.

**14-day MVP:** Python + OpenStreetMap/Overpass POI rules and a 50-location manually labeled sample; output CSV and HTML audit. Interview 8 developers and invite 5 to test in a sandbox. Do not collect private location data.

**Success:** >=3 complete trials; >=2 repeated uses or integration requests; restricted-place recall >=90%; false exclusions <=10%. **Stop:** <2 repeated uses or existing SDK performs as well at lower cost.

**Counterevidence:** issue may be rare, local or already solved by map vendors. One user's complaint is not prevalence evidence. No PMF or paid adoption verified.
