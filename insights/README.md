# Insights: User Needs and Startup Research

This directory contains cross-product synthesis grounded in publicly verifiable evidence. It does not repeat daily news.

## Research categories

- **user-needs/**: unmet jobs to be done, repeated pain points and customer workarounds.
- **product-patterns/**: interaction, distribution, retention and monetization mechanisms.
- **market-gaps/**: underserved segments, weak alternatives and market opportunity maps.
- **startup-hypotheses/**: falsifiable product ideas and low-cost MVP experiments.
- **validation/**: evidence logs, alternative explanations, counterexamples and revisions.

Each insight should include a stable ID, observation date, supporting URLs, relevant products, user segment, hypothesis, confidence, falsification criterion and the next research action.

Only independent public market research belongs here. No employer-specific project suggestions, internal analysis, private data or confidential material.
