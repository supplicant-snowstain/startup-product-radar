# Research Methodology

## Discovery channels

Product Hunt, Show HN, Indie Hackers, Y Combinator, GitHub, app stores, founder publications, independent communities, Reddit, official announcements, investor portfolios and funding disclosures. Search the past 24–72 hours for new launches, updates and meaningful customer evidence. Cover funded and bootstrapped companies.

## Venture capital discovery

Use public portfolios and announcements from Sequoia, a16z, Accel, Lightspeed, Benchmark, Index Ventures, General Catalyst, Khosla Ventures, YC and regional funds. Track investor, primary source URL, round/stage/amount if disclosed, announcement date, investment date when distinguishable and underlying product. Do not infer customer demand, retention or product-market fit from funding.

## Evidence labels

- OBSERVED: dated, traceable primary source or user observation.
- CLAIMED: founder, investor or marketing statement.
- INFERRED: analyst interpretation supported by stated assumptions.
- UNKNOWN: insufficient evidence or unverified information.
- CONTRADICTED: competing or negative evidence.

Record source URL, source publication date, event date and accessed date where possible. Avoid unsupported growth or financial figures.

## Selection criteria

Each day target 5–8 deep cases and 5–10 short candidates, but never manufacture cases to meet a quota. Favor unmet needs, distinct workflows, early real usage, iteration signals and evidence quality. Prevent survivor bias by studying failures and overlooked bootstrapped products.

## Knowledge lifecycle

1. Archive discovery under reports/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
2. Create/update products/<slug>.md for longitudinal facts.
3. Create founder profiles only for verified public journeys.
4. Extract evidence-based cross-product insights rather than repeating product profiles.
5. Maintain index/products.csv without duplicates.
6. Review hypotheses and counterexamples weekly.

## Public scope

All outputs must be independently sourced public-market research. Exclude any employer-specific internal details, privileged business analysis, confidential source material or private information. No company-specific operational recommendations in this repository.

## Synchronization

Only report success when a GitHub write succeeded and a commit was returned. If a write is unavailable or fails, disclose the issue and identify what was not archived.
